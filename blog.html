The Legal FrameworkDer rechtliche Rahmen

Students holding a German student visa (§16b AufenthG) are permitted to work up to 120 full days or 240 half days per year. A Nebengewerbe sits in a legally nuanced position — self-employed income doesn't automatically count toward the 120-day limit the same way employed work does, but interpretation varies between different Ausländerbehörde offices. The safest approach: register, notify your Ausländerbehörde, and consult a Steuerberater.Studenten mit deutschem Studentenvisum (§16b AufenthG) dürfen bis zu 120 volle Tage oder 240 halbe Tage pro Jahr arbeiten. Selbstständiges Einkommen zählt nicht automatisch auf die 120-Tage-Grenze an.

FAQ

Does running a Nebengewerbe affect my student visa?+

It depends on your specific visa type and your local Ausländerbehörde's interpretation. Many offices treat self-employed activity differently from employed work. Always notify your Ausländerbehörde in writing and get their position in writing before generating significant income.

Do I pay taxes on my Nebengewerbe income?+

Yes, but with significant relief. The German income tax basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag) is approximately €11,784/year (2024). If your total income stays below this, you pay zero income tax.

Freiberufler vs Selbstständige — Core Distinction

Freiberufler (liberal profession) are professionals in recognised categories (§18 EStG): lawyers, doctors, architects, engineers, artists, writers, designers, IT consultants, translators, teachers. No trade licence (Gewerbe) needed. No trade tax (Gewerbesteuer). Selbstständige (self-employed commercial) run businesses requiring a Gewerbe — ecommerce sellers, agency owners, service businesses not in the Freiberufler categories.Freiberufler sind Fachleute in anerkannten Kategorien (§18 EStG). Selbstständige betreiben Gewerbebetriebe, die ein Gewerbe erfordern — Ecommerce-Verkäufer und Agenturinhaber.

Application Requirements

  • Proof of qualification — University degree, professional portfolio, or industry certifications
  • Client evidence — Signed contracts, letters of intent, or invoices showing real or imminent work
  • Income projections — Business plan showing €2,000+/month net income at minimum
  • Health insurance — Comprehensive coverage mandatory before permit is issued
  • Proof of accommodation — German Anmeldung registration certificate

Processing Times by City

Berlin: 10–16 weeks — appointments booked months in advance. Munich: 4–8 weeks — more organised system. Hamburg: 6–10 weeks. Frankfurt: 5–9 weeks. Book your appointment as early as possible — ideally before your current visa expires.Berlin: 10–16 Wochen. München: 4–8 Wochen. Hamburg: 6–10 Wochen. Frankfurt: 5–9 Wochen.

German Legal Requirements for Shopify Stores

Before worrying about traffic or products, your Shopify store needs to be legally compliant for Germany. Missing these exposes you to Abmahnungen (legal warnings) from competitors or consumer protection organisations, which can cost thousands in legal fees.

1. Impressum

Every commercial website in Germany must have an Impressum page with: your full name and address, contact details including a phone number or functional email, your Gewerbe registration number, and VAT ID if registered. Use the generator at e-recht24.de for a legally correct version.

2. Widerrufsrecht (Right of Withdrawal)

EU consumer law gives buyers 14 days to return purchases without reason. Your store must display a Widerrufsbelehrung and provide a standardised Widerrufsformular. Shopify's built-in tools don't fully handle this for Germany — use a German-specific app or legal plugin.

3. GDPR Cookie Consent

Opt-in consent (not just a notice) is required for all non-essential cookies, including analytics and Facebook Pixel. Use Cookiebot, Usercentrics, or Shopify's consent solution configured correctly for Germany. Pre-checked boxes are not compliant.

4. German Payment Methods

German shoppers prefer: SEPA bank transfer (via Mollie or Stripe), PayPal (essential — 35–40% of sales), Klarna (buy now, pay later — trusted by German consumers). Configure Shopify Payments with Mollie for the most complete German payment coverage.

5. VAT Display

German law requires prices to be displayed including VAT (Bruttopreise) with the VAT amount clearly stated in checkout. "Prices excl. VAT" is only acceptable in B2B contexts. Configure Shopify tax settings to display inclusive prices from day one.

What is the Kleinunternehmerregelung?Was ist die Kleinunternehmerregelung?+
The Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG) exempts small businesses from charging and remitting VAT if turnover in the previous year was below approximately €22,000 and is not expected to exceed €50,000 in the current year.
