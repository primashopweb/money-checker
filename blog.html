FAQ — eBay Dropshipping Germany

Is dropshipping allowed on eBay Germany?+

Yes, dropshipping is permitted on eBay.de provided you can fulfil orders within the shipping timeframe stated in your listing. You are responsible for the transaction regardless of your supplier's performance. eBay penalises late dispatch — your supplier's delays become your problem.

How much capital do I need to start eBay dropshipping?+

Minimum: €50–€100 (Gewerbe registration + initial eBay selling fees). Many sellers start with zero inventory cost — you collect payment from the buyer before ordering from your supplier. Cash flow positive from day one if you choose this model carefully.

Do I need a German bank account?+

eBay Payments (the built-in payment system) pays out to a bank account. A German IBAN is strongly recommended for smooth operation — N26, DKB, or Commerzbank all work well. Non-German accounts are technically accepted but can trigger additional verification.

The Legal Framework for Student EntrepreneursDer rechtliche Rahmen für studentische Unternehmer

Students holding a German student visa (§16b AufenthG) are generally permitted to work up to 120 full days or 240 half days per year. A Nebengewerbe — a registered side business — sits in a legally nuanced position: self-employed income doesn't automatically count toward the 120-day work limit the same way employed work does, but the interpretation varies between different Ausländerbehörde offices. The safest approach: register a Nebengewerbe, notify your Ausländerbehörde, and consult a Steuerberater before making significant income-related decisions.Studenten mit deutschem Studentenvisum (§16b AufenthG) dürfen generell bis zu 120 volle Tage oder 240 halbe Tage pro Jahr arbeiten. FAQ — Student Business Germany

Does running a Nebengewerbe affect my student visa?+

It depends on your specific visa type and the interpretation of your local Ausländerbehörde. Many offices treat self-employed activity differently from employed work, meaning the 120-day limit may not apply in the same way. Always notify your Ausländerbehörde in writing and get their position in writing before generating significant income.

Do I pay taxes on my Nebengewerbe income?+

Yes, but with significant relief. The German income tax basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag) is approximately €11,784/year (2024). If your total income stays below this — combining student job income and Nebengewerbe income — you pay zero income tax. Above this threshold, progressive rates apply starting at 14%.

Freiberufler vs Selbstständige — The Core Distinction

Freiberufler (liberal profession freelancers) are professionals in recognised categories defined by §18 EStG: lawyers, doctors, architects, engineers, artists, writers, designers, journalists, scientists, IT consultants, translators, teachers, and similar fields. They do not need a trade licence (Gewerbe) and are not subject to trade tax (Gewerbesteuer). Selbstständige (self-employed commercial operators) run commercial businesses requiring a Gewerbe — this includes ecommerce sellers, agency owners, service businesses not in the Freiberufler categories, and anyone selling physical products. They pay Gewerbesteuer above certain thresholds.Freiberufler sind Fachleute in anerkannten Kategorien gemäß §18 EStG. Selbstständige betreiben Gewerbebetriebe, die ein Gewerbe erfordern — dazu gehören Ecommerce-Verkäufer und Agenturinhaber.

FAQ — Freelancer Visa Germany

Can I apply for a freelancer visa if I don't have clients yet?+

Technically, letters of intent from potential clients may be accepted — but this depends heavily on the Ausländerbehörde officer reviewing your case. In practice, having at least one signed contract or invoiced project significantly improves your chances. Consider working remotely for a foreign employer first while building your German client base.

Can I hire employees on a freelancer visa?+

As a Freiberufler, employing staff is restricted — you're expected to be the expert doing the work, not a business owner managing others. As a Selbstständige with a Gewerbe, hiring is possible but requires additional permits and compliance with German employment law. Most self-employed visa holders start solo and hire later once they establish GmbH status.

The German Legal Requirements for Shopify Stores

Before worrying about traffic or products, your Shopify store needs to be legally compliant for the German market. Missing these isn't just bad practice — it exposes you to Abmahnungen (legal warnings) from competitors or consumer protection organisations, which can cost thousands in legal fees.

1. Impressum

Every commercial website in Germany must have an Impressum page with: your full name and address (no PO boxes), contact details including a phone number or functional email, your Gewerbe registration number, and VAT ID if you're registered. Use the Impressum generator from e-recht24.de to create a legally correct version.

2. Widerrufsrecht (Right of Withdrawal)

EU consumer law gives buyers 14 days to return purchases without reason. Your Shopify store must display a Widerrufsbelehrung (withdrawal notice) and provide a standardised Widerrufsformular (withdrawal form). Shopify's built-in tools don't fully handle this for Germany — you'll need a German-specific app or legal plugin.

3. GDPR Cookie Consent

German data protection authorities interpret GDPR cookie rules strictly — opt-in consent (not just a notice) is required for all non-essential cookies, including Shopify analytics, Facebook Pixel, and Google Analytics. Use Cookiebot, Usercentrics, or Shopify's own consent solution configured correctly for Germany. Pre-checked boxes and "continue browsing" acceptance are not compliant.

4. German Payment Methods

German shoppers prefer: SEPA bank transfer (via Mollie or Stripe), PayPal (essential — expect 35–40% of sales to use it), Klarna (buy now, pay later — trusted by German consumers), and Kreditkarte (lower percentage than in US/UK). Configure Shopify Payments with Mollie for the most complete German payment coverage. Avoid stores that only accept credit cards — you'll lose a significant portion of German buyers.

5. VAT Display

German law requires prices to be displayed including VAT (Bruttopreise) with the VAT amount clearly stated in the checkout. "Prices excl. VAT" is only acceptable in B2B contexts. Configure your Shopify tax settings to display inclusive prices in Germany from day one.

FAQ — Shopify Germany

Can I use Shopify's standard legal templates for Germany?+

No. Shopify's built-in legal templates are written for US/UK markets and are not compliant with German consumer protection law. Use IT-Recht Kanzlei, Händlerbund, or e-recht24.de for professionally maintained German-market legal texts — these services update their templates when laws change.

Which Shopify plan do I need for a German store?+

The Basic plan (€29/month) is sufficient for most new German stores. Only move to Shopify plan (€79/month) when your transaction volume makes the lower transaction fees (2% → 1%) save you more than the €50 plan difference. Most German entrepreneurs start on Basic and scale from there.

Do I need a Gewerbe for side hustle income in Germany?+

If your activity is commercial (selling products, providing services regularly) rather than occasional private selling, a Gewerbe is required. The registration costs €20–€40 and is done in 20 minutes at your local Gewerbeamt. Failing to register when required is a regulatory violation. If your activity qualifies as Freiberuflich (freelance in a liberal profession), you don't need a Gewerbe but must still register with the Finanzamt.

How much can I earn before paying tax in Germany?+

The income tax basic allowance (Grundfreibetrag) is approximately €11,784/year (2024). Income below this from all sources combined is tax-free. Above this, progressive rates start at 14%. Note: the Kleinunternehmerregelung (small business exemption) for VAT is separate — it applies to turnovers below ~€22,000/year and exempts you from charging and collecting VAT, simplifying your accounting significantly.

How do I start eBay dropshipping in Germany with no money?+
The closest to zero-cost start: (1) Register a Nebengewerbe (€20–€40 — this is necessary). (2) Open a free eBay.de business account. (3) Use the "order-after-payment" model — list products at your price, order from your supplier only after the customer pays you. This means you're never out of pocket on inventory. The key risk: your supplier's delivery time must match your listing's stated timeframe. Start with suppliers with EU warehouse stock and 3–5 day delivery. What is the Kleinunternehmerregelung and do I qualify?+
The Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG) exempts small businesses from charging and remitting VAT if their turnover in the previous calendar year was below approximately €22,000 and is not expected to exceed €50,000 in the current year (as of the 2024 reform). If you qualify, you: don't charge VAT on your invoices, don't file VAT returns, and have simpler accounting. The tradeoff: you can't reclaim input VAT on business expenses. For most early-stage ecommerce sellers, the simplicity benefit outweighs the input VAT disadvantage. Inform your Finanzamt on the tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen) that you're applying the Kleinunternehmerregelung.Die Kleinunternehmerregelung (§19 UStG) befreit kleine Unternehmen von der Umsatzsteuererhebung und -abführung, wenn ihr Umsatz im Vorjahr unter ca. 22.000 € lag.
Can I run an ecommerce business while working full-time in Germany?+
Yes, with conditions. Check your employment contract for exclusivity or non-compete clauses — some contracts prohibit side businesses in the same industry. If your contract is silent on this, a Nebengewerbe in an unrelated field is generally permissible. Inform your employer if required by your contract. Also note: income from a Nebengewerbe stacks with your employment income for tax purposes — if you're in the 42% marginal tax bracket from employment, your side business profit above that threshold is taxed at 42% plus solidarity surcharge.Ja, mit Bedingungen.
