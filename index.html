How to start an online business in Germany as a foreigner or student

Germany is one of the strongest ecommerce markets in Europe, with over €100 billion in annual online retail sales. Whether you're an international student, a newly arrived freelancer, or a remote entrepreneur, building an online income stream from Germany is entirely legal — provided you follow the correct registration and tax procedures.

eBay Dropshipping in Germany — What You Need to Know

eBay Germany (eBay.de) remains one of the most accessible marketplaces for new sellers in Europe. Dropshipping is permitted provided you can fulfil orders within the promised timeframe. You'll need a registered business (Gewerbe), a German VAT number if turnover exceeds the Kleinunternehmer threshold, and a clear return policy compliant with EU consumer law.

Freelancer Visa Germany (Selbstständige) — Key Facts

Non-EU nationals can apply for a German freelancer visa or self-employment permit (Aufenthaltserlaubnis zur Ausübung einer selbstständigen Tätigkeit). Requirements include proof of professional qualifications, existing client relationships or contracts, sufficient income projections, and health insurance. Processing typically takes 4–8 weeks through the local Ausländerbehörde.

Ecommerce for International Students in Germany

Students holding a German student visa (Aufenthaltserlaubnis zum Studium) are generally permitted to work up to 120 full days or 240 half days per year. Operating a small Nebengewerbe (side business) for online sales, freelance services, or digital products typically falls within these boundaries — but always confirm with your local Ausländerbehörde and a Steuerberater.

VPS Hosting for German Ecommerce — Why Location Matters

Under GDPR, customer data must be processed within the EU. Hosting your WooCommerce, Shopify alternative, or custom ecommerce application on a server located in Germany (Frankfurt) or the EU ensures compliance, reduces latency for German shoppers, and avoids data transfer complications. A VPS gives you dedicated resources so your store doesn't slow down during traffic spikes — a common issue on shared hosting that directly impacts conversion rates.

FAQ

Common questions about online business & ecommerce

Straight answers to the questions we get asked most often.

Can international students start an online business in Germany? +
Yes. International students in Germany can register a Nebengewerbe (side business) at the local Gewerbeamt and earn income through ecommerce, freelancing, or digital services. Most student visas permit up to 120 full working days per year. Always verify your specific visa conditions and consult a Steuerberater for tax guidance.
What is the German freelancer visa (Selbstständige) and who qualifies? +
The German self-employment visa allows non-EU nationals to live and work in Germany as freelancers or business owners. Eligibility depends on your profession, demonstrable qualifications, existing client relationships or contracts, income projections, and health insurance. Creative professionals, IT specialists, engineers, and consultants are commonly approved. Applications are submitted at the Ausländerbehörde in your city.
How do I start eBay dropshipping in Germany? +
To start eBay dropshipping in Germany: (1) Register a Gewerbe at your local Gewerbeamt, (2) Open an eBay business seller account, (3) Find reliable suppliers on AliExpress, CJ Dropshipping, or European wholesalers, (4) List products with compliant descriptions and shipping times, (5) Handle VAT correctly — Kleinunternehmer exemption applies under ~€22,000 annual turnover. For a fully managed approach, platforms offering managed ecommerce support can handle the entire process.
What VPS hosting is best for ecommerce stores in Germany? +
For German ecommerce, choose a VPS with an EU or Frankfurt data centre for GDPR compliance and low latency to German shoppers. Minimum specs for a growing WooCommerce store: 4 GB RAM, 2 vCPU, NVMe SSD storage, and DDoS protection. Avoid shared hosting for serious stores — traffic spikes can take your site offline during peak sales periods.
What are the best side hustles in Germany in 2025? +
Top performing side hustles in Germany in 2025: eBay reselling and dropshipping, Etsy print-on-demand (via Printful or Printify), freelance translation or proofreading (strong demand in DE→EN), content creation on YouTube or a niche blog, affiliate marketing, and online tutoring. Most can be started for under €100 and run part-time alongside full-time employment or studies.
How do I set up a Shopify store for the German market? +
Setting up Shopify for Germany requires: DSGVO-compliant privacy policy and cookie consent, German language store (Shopify Language app), local payment methods (PayPal, Klarna, SEPA via Mollie or Stripe), proper Impressum and Widerrufsrecht pages, correct VAT display (19% standard, 7% reduced), and EU return policy. Use a theme with clean mobile performance — Core Web Vitals directly affect Google rankings.
